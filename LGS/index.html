About Us

Welcome to
League Global School, Bengaluru

League Global School is recognised by the Government of Karnataka to offer English medium education from nursery to 10th standard. We at League Global School believe, if students learn right, they will naturally become smart. Hence, we have created the infrastructure and environment at our School for learning. Our School is the place for students to fully explore their potential to grow.

League Global School is a new age co-educational institution promoted by SevaGyan trust founded by Dr. G P Naik, who is a teacher by profession. Our aim is to provide high quality education for the all-round development of students to become better citizens by accomplishing their life goals. We give importance to all the subjects of learning along with emphasis on sports, yoga, arts, literature, social service as well as life skills training. We strive to give individual attention to the learning needs of each and every student. We emphasise the learning of critical subjects like Science, Mathematics, English and Computer Science.

Goutam Buddha says “Words have the power both to destroy and heal. When words are true and kind they can change our world”. Hence we give individual attention to the learning needs of each and every student admitted to our School, so that they not only learn better but they talk smart and walk right. Please do visit our School to explore, how we can facilitate learning in every student.

Strategic Location

League Global School is strategically located in the IT corridor of Bengaluru on the Bannerghatta- Jigani Road and connected by BMTC buses. Students may also avail school bus facility.

Academics & Teachers

Academics

Learning activities get utmost importance in our school. We focus on quality in each and every aspect of our educational curriculum. Academic performance of our students is monitored by continuous assessment of the learning outcome. Students are given additional coaching to overcome the learning difficulties.

Our Teachers

We at League Global School have got a carefully chosen team of teachers led by an eminent Principal with proven experience in their respective subjects to teach rightly. They are also trained to mentor the students needing learning assistance. All the teachers at our School undergo mandatory training from time to time to upgrade their subject knowledge, teaching skills and behaviour.

Facilities

Outdoor and indoor sports, yoga, arts, literature, community service and other extracurricular activities for all-round development of students.

  • Well stacked Library, Science lab, Computer Lab and internet connectivity for study and practice.
  • Spacious classrooms for effective learning.
  • Experienced teachers led by eminent Principal to teach and mentor the students.
  • Free tutorials to learn difficult subjects.
  • Life skills training.
  • Transportation facility.
  • Playground for sports and games.
  • Field visits and outward-bound learning.

Kindergarten

We have the most vibrant and engaging Kindergarten at League Global School. Our Kindergarten is fully equipped with qualified teachers, modern learning aids and toys to promote new age learning. Kindergarten classes are conducted in spacious classrooms with lot of space for activity-based learning.

Primary School

Our primary school is abuzz with variety of learning activities like classroom teaching, field visits, activity-based learning, laboratory experiments, library sessions, yoga, sports, cultural activities, field trips, public speaking sessions along with strong emphasis on mathematics, science and languages.

High School

On entering the High School, students will be sensitised about the need for high degree of effort coupled with continuous motivation to learn all the six subjects. Accelerated learning, revision of lessons, special classes, practice sessions, and simulation tests are some of the hallmarks of our success story. Our school has the reputation of getting 100% results right since inception.

Admission Procedure

Admissions are open for students from Nursery to 10th standard. Those who want admission may visit our school or contact us by email leagueglobalschool@gmail.com or by phone 7975638721 / 9243470110 / 7892580571.

Minimum Age

  • 3 years for Nursery
  • 4 years for LKG
  • 5 years for UKG
  • 6 years for First Standard as on 31st May
Admission for other classes will be based on Transfer Certificate (TC) issued by the previous school.

Documents Needed

  • Birth certificate (Original & Photocopy)
  • Aadhar card (Original & Photocopy)
  • 5 passport-size photos
  • Aadhar card of parents
  • Original TC (For 2nd standard & above)
