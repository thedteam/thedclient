Welcome to Mana‑na

Manana means reflective thinking — the process of understanding one’s thoughts with clarity. This timeless concept is the heartbeat of our practice, reminding us that mental well‑being is deeply connected to how we live, grow, and thrive.

Our Approach

At Mana‑na, we believe that awareness and emotional resilience are the foundations of a fulfilling life. More than methods, we offer companionship on your journey:

  • Compassionate Care — A safe, non‑judgmental space where you feel seen and valued.
  • Evidence‑Based Practice — Proven scientific methods that empower your healing journey.
  • Practical Support — Tools for personal growth you can apply in everyday life.

Your Journey, Supported

We are here to help you navigate your story with clarity and grace. Whether you are seeking guidance through a difficult phase or looking for the different perspective to flourish, Mana‑na walks alongside you.

Our mission is simple: to make mental health support practical, accessible, and deeply personal.

Meet the Counselor

Hi, I am Divya Narayan

I believe therapy should feel like a safe, supportive space where you can explore challenges and discover new ways forward. I am a counselling psychologist with a Master’s in Counselling Psychology and a Diploma in Counselling Skills, trained in CBT-informed, evidence-based approaches.

I work with adolescents and adults experiencing stress, anxiety, emotional concerns, relationship difficulties, and life transitions. Having spent years in high-pressure academic and leadership environments, I understand the stress and expectations that often come with them. I use those insights to help clients build practical coping strategies that fit their unique lives.

My approach is person-centered and evidence-based, drawing on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (REBT). Together, we’ll work to shift patterns of thought and behavior that no longer serve you, creating space for resilience, clarity, and growth.
