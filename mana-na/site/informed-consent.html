Psychotherapy Services Agreement

Welcome to Mana-na Counselling & Wellbeing Space. This document provides important information about our professional services and business policies. When you sign this document, it will also represent an agreement between us. Please read it carefully.

1. The Nature of Therapy

Therapy is a collaborative relationship between a psychotherapist and a client. While it can result in significant benefits such as better relationships, solutions to specific problems, and significant reductions in feelings of distress, it also requires your active involvement, honesty, and openness.

Since therapy often involves discussing unpleasant aspects of your life, you may experience uncomfortable feelings like sadness, guilt, anger, frustration, or helplessness. However, therapy has been shown to have benefits for individuals who go through it.

2. Confidentiality

Trust is the foundation of the therapeutic relationship. All communications between a client and a psychotherapist, as well as your records, are strictly confidential. However, there are some legal and ethical exceptions to this rule:

  • If we assess that there is an imminent risk of harm to yourself or to another person, we are legally required to take protective actions (e.g., notifying emergency contacts or relevant authorities).
  • If we suspect or receive information about the abuse or neglect of a minor, elderly person, or vulnerable adult.
  • If records are court-ordered by a judge in legal proceedings.

3. Appointments & Cancellations

Consistency is vital to progress in therapy. Once an appointment hour is scheduled, that time is reserved exclusively for you. If you must cancel or reschedule an appointment, we require a minimum of 24 hours advance notice.

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments (no-shows), limit our ability to provide care to others and may be subject to a cancellation fee.

4. Emergency & Crisis Protocol

Mana-na provides scheduled psychological services and is not an emergency center. We do not offer 24/7 on-call crisis support.

If you are experiencing a severe psychiatric crisis, intense suicidal thoughts, or a medical emergency, you must immediately contact your nearest hospital or 24/7 crisis helplines, such as Tele MANAS at 14416 or KIRAN at 1800-599-0019.

5. Client Rights

You have the right to ask questions about your treatment process, refuse any therapeutic intervention, and discuss alternative treatment options. You may also choose to terminate therapy at any time, although we recommend having a closure session to summarize your progress.
