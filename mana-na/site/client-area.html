How It Works

Starting therapy can sometimes feel intimidating, but it doesn’t have to be. We’ve broken down the process into four simple steps so you know exactly what to expect.

1

Reach Out

Take the first step. Use our contact form, send an email, or book a slot to express your interest in starting therapy.

2

Initial Consultation

We'll schedule a brief 15-minute chat. This allows us to understand your needs and see if we are a good fit for working together.

3

First Session

In our first full session, we'll conduct an initial assessment, understand your background, and begin setting meaningful goals.

4

Ongoing Support

We'll continue with tailored, evidence-based therapy sessions to support your personal growth, resilience, and wellbeing.

5

Progress Review

Periodically, we'll reflect on your progress, celebrate milestones, and adjust our approach to ensure it meets your evolving needs.

6

Moving Forward

As you achieve your goals, we'll equip you with lasting tools and strategies to maintain your wellbeing and thrive independently.
